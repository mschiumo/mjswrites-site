there’s a softness to the world that i find from time to time,  
most often when i am not searching for it  

it is a gentle reminder that the hearts around me, be they enshrined in enigmatic scars or crumpled beyond recognition, are delicate, and deserving of care  
i hope to be with them, to share what i may  

for, in this life, we all yearn for the same, yet each find our own difficulties in achieving it  

but having a sensitive spirit and a serene soul makes living in the softness of the world  
a beautiful experience shared by all, experienced differently by each

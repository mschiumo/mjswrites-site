Today, watching the bell ring as Dario Amodei and a group of elated Anthropic executives made their initial public offering at a record valuation, I had the eerie sense that life had changed forever. I know that this might sound dramatic, and that not everyone follows the stock market like I do (I know, very nerdy of me…). However, I feel that this acknowledgement of a shift in reality is not without justification.

To start things off, Anthropic is the first ‘Frontier Lab’ in the world of Artificial Intelligence to go public (OpenAI is currently waiting in the wings ominously for their own IPO in coming months). The timing seemingly couldn’t be more chaotic - the debacle with Jensen Huang and Nvidia just days before today’s IPO, a raging war in the Middle East, a sliding market that seems to have taken every asset class down with it in recent weeks, and a stark warning from a litany of voices in the Tech space that unleashing this technology yields a very real chance of posing an existential threat to humanity all coalesced into what can only be described as utter chaos. Still, within minutes of $ANT trading on the market, the company officially crossed the threshold to reach a staggering $2 trillion market cap, minting thousands of millionaires among the eager cohort of Anthropic employees fortunate enough to participate in this colossal wealth creation.

## An Industry Divided

  <img src="/images/karen-hao.webp" alt="Author Karen Hao" />
  <figcaption>Author Karen Hao</figcaption>
</figure>

There appears to be no sound consensus of what this truly spells for the future - the two camps have been divided into the ‘Boomers’ and the ‘Doomers,’ as [award-winning writer Karen Hao puts it in her book](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=ai+empires&crid=36TQNN2OUIBRW&sprefix=ai+emp%2Caps%2C268&ref=nb_sb_noss_2), *Empire of AI: Dreams and Nightmares in Sam Altman’s OpenAI.* The first group, the ‘Boomers,’ firmly believe that we have officially entered a new paradigm in which this world-altering technology will usher in an incomprehensible tsunami of productivity, efficiency, and the ability to grapple with the world’s greatest crises, including cancer and climate change. The ‘Doomers,’ on the other hand, are resolute in their belief that we have opened Pandora’s box, effectively unleashing a dangerous technology, which we don’t fully understand and which can be easily weaponized, to nefarious actors around the world. I tend to side with the latter.

As if for intentional dramatic effect, President Trump is currently in Asia with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin, two of America’s greatest adversaries on the global stage at the moment, especially when considering the former is the single largest competitor in the AI Race as it stands currently. A large push from the AI industry, even from the early days of OpenAI when it was a simple non-profit looking to channel technology to help humanity, has been the constant touting of the ‘threat’ that China poses in the desperate clamor to reach ‘Superintelligence.’ They have argued that, similar to the Space Race, the first nation to reach this milestone will effectively dominate geopolitics in the coming decades as the power to scale Artificial Intelligence, alongside Robotics, rapidly rises. Even Anthropic’s CEO has come out many times, both in writing and in front of the camera, to warn of the dangers of his own product, including [in its own IPO filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026072484/anthropicpbc-sx1ax2.htm). Still, efforts to rein in the enthusiasm, sheer pace, and amount of capital flying into this industry have remained unsuccessful, even as major cracks across credit, circular financing, and growing uncertainty surrounding the viability of AI as a profitable business model have risen to the surface.

Following a series of inexplicable outages in major communications and software across the United States, including FAA shutdowns and banking-access scares, the public, it seems, is more aware than ever that AI is no longer a topic of discussion - it is a new and unavoidable reality. It is no longer hyperbole to say that the future of our economy, society, and all that we value as a species is on the hook, anxiously waiting for the bubble to pop, or soar to new heights. Anthropic’s entrance onto the stage as a major global power marks the beginning of rapid changes to the way that we view employment, wealth inequality, fraud and abuse, and the role of government in protecting ordinary people from [the whims of a handful of ‘Broligarchs’ looking to play God.](/blog/can-we-trust-the-broligarchs)

What will be of the most vital importance in coming years is the focus on how we respond to the inevitable crises of mass unemployment, AI psychosis, and the fact that the wealthy elite have never had a stronger grip on the course of humanity’s future. For now, I have one suggestion - be careful which email you let Claude connect to, because [it might just be a matter of life and death](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/anthropic-warns-ai-may-pose-existential-risks-humanity-ipo-filing-2026-09-29/).

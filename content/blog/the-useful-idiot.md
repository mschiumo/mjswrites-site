The Soviets had a term to describe a person who was easily manipulated by propaganda to unknowingly advance an agenda — a ‘useful idiot.’ Who could have imagined that, less than 30 years after the collapse of the Union, one would be elected President of the United States?

Quite frankly, I am exhausted by the perpetual charade maintained by Trump supporters that he is anything but one of the most inept, crooked, and corrupt individuals to ever hold public office in the United States.

There is no master plan.

There is no 4D chess.

There is no tactical maneuvering.

Trump is the ‘useful idiot’ of the world, perpetually called to act in the interests of only two parties — himself, and whoever is bribing and/or stroking his ego in that moment.

This comes as no surprise, considering that Trump and his family have been in the spotlight for decades. He is the quintessential narcissist with a dangerous addition — his father’s wealth. Through a career of scamming, defrauding, and shameless self-promotion, Trump was able to ‘fail upwards,’ accruing wealth mainly on the back of his public persona rather than any discernible business acumen.

People far more adept than him realized that Trump could be bought for a price. In fact, it has only become clearer in his second term as president — the wealthiest men on the planet lined up behind him during his inauguration in a disturbing display of adulation, cognizant of the fact that their ‘donations’ to his campaign, ballroom, and more would curry favor, and give them a free pass to bend the law to their will.

Nothing exemplifies this more than the current scandal with Nvidia’s Jensen Huang. Disregarding intelligence that suggested that Jensen committed not only adultery and significant ethics violations within Nvidia, but what could amount to treason and betrayal of the United States, Trump was more than happy to pull strings to [reinstate him as CEO](/blog/return-of-the-jensen). As is always the case, if you want to understand his motivation, follow the money. Trump owned millions in Nvidia stock, and likely made a small fortune following the surge in price as a result of the news of Jensen’s return.

The case of Trump being used for his gullibility and lack of morals extends beyond our borders, as well. Putin, whom Trump affectionately calls his ‘friend,’ has successfully influenced US policy decisions related to the war in Ukraine numerous times, and his ‘strongman’ image seems to leave Trump perpetually swooning. Xi Jinping also seems capable of manipulating Trump, whose claims of being ‘tough on China’ have yet to amount to anything other than a failed attempt to levy unconstitutional tariffs against them. Just recently, Trump seemed enticed by the idea of implementing universal healthcare simply because Senator-elect Abdul El-Sayed facetiously suggested naming it ‘TrumpCare.’ Clearly, it is irresponsible to allow a person of such character to act on behalf of the United States, in any capacity.

It is beyond obvious that this man does not want to govern. Truthfully, he is incapable of it. He simply wants to stand in front of people and ramble senselessly for hours without being interrupted, see his face on television, receive undeserved praise from his sycophantic inner circle and opportunists, and pillage this country for all it’s worth, as long as he and his cronies are benefiting.

Before his tenure as President is over, Trump will have led this country into a quagmire of unmitigated corruption, devastating economic collapse, and unrelenting shame.

I dream in the flickers of a flame  

And hold my breath, lest my wistful sighs extinguish them  

Will I ever find peace in this world, or am I destined to suffer alongside it?  

I can say that I have not let my spirit break, at least not entirely  

There still remains a fragment that wishes to endure, to see the change that I know must come  

I must choose a path now, for the embers are seething  

They threaten to leave me in the darkness, in the wake of their departure  

I shall close my eyes, and imagine a world in which the light never falters,  

But simply flickers with the flames of hope

would you forget the world for a moment with me?  

let’s lose ourselves in passion instead, as if that were all that truly mattered  

leave the sun to sink in the sky, and the moon to wallow in the heavens  

in this moment, they do not concern us  

i wish only for your touch, for the gentle sigh as your worries melt away

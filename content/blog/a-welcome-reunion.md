i’ve been inspired by humanity recently  
it was a welcome reunion  
to feel that there is the potential for a shift, that there are still people among us that wake with the intention to make change  
it feeds my soul in ways i cannot explain  
i have always known that good exists in this world  
it just happens to have a proclivity to be overwritten by the bad  
but it is there, and even if it only existed within me, i hold no doubt that i would find the fortitude to plant it within the earth once more  
from hope flourishes change, and in a world ever spinning, i find reason to continue  
in moments of trial, this inspiration never ceases to push me onward

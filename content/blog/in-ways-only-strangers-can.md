i should have kissed you  

it had been on my mind all night  

the desire only grew as your laughter enchanted me  

as we spoke of our lives in ways only strangers can  

i could feel that neither of us wanted the night to come to a close  

we longed for it to drag on endlessly  

to bless us with the luxury of nowhere to be but in each other’s presence  

the cityscape became the backdrop, but it was your eyes that remained the center of my attention  

i simply couldn’t look away  

there was something so…enticing within them  

that i long for them still, knowing all too well that the miles between us are beyond measure  

i find myself drifting to sleep most nights, thinking only of regret - i should have found the courage….  

i should have kissed you.

i miss the days before i knew so much about climate change  

when i believed that the trees swayed just the same on any old day  

i miss the days before i had to declare myself a poet  

when it was just something that poured from my soul without a purpose or a need for attention  

i miss the days when life seemed simpler  

not because it was easier, but because it was slower  

there are many days to miss, and many moments lost forever  

so, i’ll have to settle for the ones to come  

because, before long, i’ll be missing them too

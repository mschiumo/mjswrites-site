the future has never been clear to me, but one thing remains certain – this undying love i hold for those who share this world with me will burn as fiercely as ever  
let us cast away our differences as though we were shedding our collective skin, and foster a new hope  
in all of the nights when sleep was but a long-lost dream, my sense of wonder has yet to be eclipsed  
how capable we are, how mighty…  
brimming with talent unparalleled,  
bursting with affection for those around,  
instilling new life in places it had yet to reach  
this is the hope i choose to hold onto  
i shall cling to it ferociously, lest it slip away  
and when my strength has waned,  
it will be my brothers and sisters who spur my weary spirit forward  
all of these boundaries and differences we have surrounded ourselves with are but petty illusion  
alas, they could never withstand the might of our convictions  
breathe deeply, my kin, for the path to our destiny will be hard-fought  
but if there were ever a goal worth reaching for, worth stretching to our limits and beyond,  
it is this one  
rest gently, for tomorrow we start anew  
finding peace was never a trivial pursuit, but a promise  
a promise i intend to see through

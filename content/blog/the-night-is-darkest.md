someone once said that the night is darkest before the dawn  

it is the moment when the path is not visible, and there remains nothing ahead to pull us forward  

but i have begun to see hope  

perhaps, the first rays of sunlight have come upon us  

let them drench us with their warmth, until inspiration flows so freely, our spirits have no choice but to rise  

because, for the first time in so long, i have begun to see hope  

and that is reason enough to believe that the sun, too, shall rise

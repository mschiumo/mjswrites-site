bury my soul in rubble, let the woes of the world cascade upon my spirit  

in this place, i find the familiar  

trample my ambitions, besiege my dreams with ceaseless doubt  

still, i reach  

my hands are outstretched, yearning for the light, encased in sorrow, and enshrined in struggle  

alas, why else have i been endowed with the strength to carry on?  

challenges become pleasures, and setbacks - opportunity  

the cloudy days may summon rains to drown my optimism  

still, i reach  

it is my heart that guides me, even when a path is nowhere to be found  

so long as i remain resolutely myself, i could never be aimless  

terror, fury, wickedness  

i dare thee to strike at me!  

still, i reach  

what damage has been done has only served to prove that it has not been sufficient to break me, in all its might  

and, in this light, there is no sense to fear what comes  

i have not reached the sacred place that i see in my mind when fitful sleep befalls me, nor do i enjoy the splendor of all that i have yet to sow upon the fruitful earth  

still, i reach

what a privilege to have the chance to describe you in ways no one ever has…  

it brings me such joy  

this city is ours for the evening, standing still as if the dawn were holding its breath just to give us a few more quiet moments  

all of the troubles, the plagues of our minds, have absconded into the shadows  

leaving your soft glow in their place  

we could sit beneath the stars all night and the desire to part would never come upon me  

because i am reluctant to leave what i have just discovered

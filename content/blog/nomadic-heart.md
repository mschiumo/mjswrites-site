although it may be enchanting, be wary of falling for a nomadic heart  

you'll find wistful memories of long-forgotten mornings lingering upon her lips,  
and longing for a far-away memory smoldering in the depths of her eyes will surely tear you apart  

you'll lose yourself hopelessly in her ponderances, just as she has lost herself so many times before  
her heart beats more fiercely with each new place conquered, as though within dwells the spirit of seafarers setting out for the distant shore  

do not dare latch onto her, for that shall be your last mistake  
she is a spirit untethered, and to defy her is to pit yourself against gravity, as well as fate  

but, if you choose not to heed my warning, then, perhaps, you shall forego this one, too  
to question why it is that the horizon calls to her is to question why it is that the sky is blue  

it is simply her nature, just as the wind sings softly to the trees  
her beauty is ethereal, her embrace - as tender and enveloping as the breeze  

her greatest allure, however, is not within her penchant to roam  
but the peculiar certainty that, wherever you should be fortunate enough to find her, begins to feel just like home  

and she'll entice you to chase her, to prance across the prairies, and traipse whimsically amongst the trees  
you'll hear the echoes of her voice amidst the birdsong, and find her sweetness decadent, like honey crafted by the bees  

if you have not the vitality, then you must simply let her go  
for the pain of watching her float away is surely nothing to the agony of knowing that you once had her for your own  

alas, she will never settle, as nomadic hearts are wont to do  
her spirit was conceived to be whisked away a thousand times, and a thousand times more - born anew

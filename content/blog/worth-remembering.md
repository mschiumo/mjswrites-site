it’s not every day that strangers walk into each other’s lives,  
but i’m grateful that you did  

it seemed too fortunate a moment, as if it were meant for someone other than me  
but once i had met your gaze, i wasn’t going to let you walk away forever  

there was something about the silkiness of your tone, and the elegance of your movements  
it drew me closer until i had become ensnared by you  

though it may have been brief, it weighed heavily upon my mind, and soon,  
i resolved to see you again  

when that day will come, even i cannot say  
but i must tell you that i look forward to uncovering that which i have yet to discover of you  

for if it even compares to your intrigue,  
it shall be a reunion worth remembering
